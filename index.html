01How do I report something I lost or found?

Click Report an Item, choose lost or found, and fill in where and when. Adding a photo makes it much easier to match.

02How do I get in touch with the finder?

Open the item and press This is mine or I found this. Fill in the short form and we pass your message on privately. They reply straight to your email.

03Is my email shown to everyone?

No. Your email is never shown on the site. Messages about your item are forwarded to you, and you decide whether to reply.

04What happens once an item is returned?

Click the private Mark as returned link in the email you got when you reported it. The item gets a Reunited stamp. Reports that aren't returned hide themselves after 30 days.
