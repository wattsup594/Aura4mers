About Lost & Found

Helping our community, one item at a time.

Lost & Found was created to make it easier for people to find their missing belongings and return found items to their rightful owners. It's a simple, community-focused board for the students, faculty, and staff at our school, built on trust, kindness, and connection.

Built by Team Aura 4mers "It's not about the journey, it's about the result."

Community Driven

We're built by and for our school. Every report helps someone get their item back.
