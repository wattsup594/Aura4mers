About Lost & Found

Helping our community, one item at a time.

Lost & Found was created to make it easier for people to find their missing belongings and return found items to their rightful owners.

We're a simple, community-focused platform built on trust, kindness, and connection.

🧭 🔑 📓 🎒 🤝
👥

Community Driven

We're built by and for our community. Every report helps someone get their item back.

🛡️

Safe & Trustworthy

We take privacy and security seriously. Your information should always be protected.

💚

A Positive Impact

Reuniting people with their belongings builds a stronger, more connected community.

Our Mission

To create a reliable and easy-to-use platform where people can report, find, and return lost items — helping our community stay connected and supported.
